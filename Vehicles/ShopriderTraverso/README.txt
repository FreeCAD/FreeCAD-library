Shoprider Traverso mobility scooter

Used as the donor vehicle in open source hardware self driving car OpenPodcar:

Camara, Fanta, Waltham, Chris, Churchill, Grey et al. (2023) OpenPodcar: An Open
Source Vehicle for Self-Driving Car Research. Journal of Open Hardware. ISSN: 2514-
1708 

The model has functional steering column, linkage and wheels, use the spreadsheet "steer" variable to turn it.
