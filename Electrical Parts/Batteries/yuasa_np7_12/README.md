From the datasheet: 151 L x 65 W x 97.5 H over terminals, terminal layout 4 (both terminals at one end),
Faston 187 tab 4.70 W x 0.80 T x 6.35 H with 1.5 x 2.5 mm slot.
Estimated from photos: case top 94 mm, 13 mm black lid, 20 mm terminal ledge 3.9 mm below lid top,
tab centres 9 mm from end and 13 mm from each side, 1 mm terminal bosses (red = +, black = -).
Origin: bottom corner under the terminal end; X = length, Y = width, Z = up. + terminal at high Y.
