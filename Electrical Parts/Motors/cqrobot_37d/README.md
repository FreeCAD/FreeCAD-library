This is an independently drawn approximation, not a manufacturer drawing. Published CQRobot
dimensions used: 37 mm dia x 70.1 mm body, 6 mm D-shaft 16 mm long, 38x30x30 mm bracket.
Assumed (typical 37D pattern, check before use): 6x M3 holes on 31 mm PCD (max 3 mm screw depth),
shaft offset 7 mm, 12 mm x 6 mm boss, 0.5 mm D-flat, gearbox/motor/encoder split 26.5/33.6/10 mm,
3 mm bracket thickness, 4 face screws, 2 M3 slots in base.
Origin: centre of gearbox front face; +Z = shaft direction; bracket base underneath (-Y).
